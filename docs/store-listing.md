# Store listing (Google Play, App Store, Microsoft Store)

**Name:** Happy PDF
**Short description (80 characters):** Every document tool. Free. Private. Nothing uploaded.

**Full description:**
Happy PDF is a complete document toolkit that works entirely on your phone or computer. Your files never leave your device: there is no account, no upload and no watermark, and it works offline.

- Merge, split, organise and compress PDFs
- Make a PDF, photo or signature fit an exact size limit for exam, job and visa forms (SSC, IBPS, UPSC, NEET, US visa, passport)
- Scan documents with your camera: edges found automatically, straightened and cleaned up
- Copy text out of scans with OCR, in English and Hindi, and make searchable PDFs
- Convert Word, Excel and PowerPoint to PDF, and PDF to Word
- Convert and resize images: JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, SVG, HEIC
- Sign, watermark, add page numbers, protect with a password, unlock
- Build an ATS-friendly resume and check its score against a job post

**Category:** Productivity
**Contact / support:** https://github.com/rohittrimukhe/Pdftools/issues

## Privacy answers
- **Google Play Data safety:** no data collected; no data shared. The app has no internet permission.
- **Apple App Privacy:** Data Not Collected.
- **Camera (iOS NSCameraUsageDescription):** "Happy PDF uses the camera only when you tap Take a photo, to scan a document. Photos stay on your device."
